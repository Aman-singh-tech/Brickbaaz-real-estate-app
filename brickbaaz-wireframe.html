Brickbaaz real estate app

Low-fidelity layout for two apps, a customer app (browse, save, contact) and a separate owner app (login, post, dashboard, inquiries), built from your six design screens (marked FROM DESIGN) plus the twelve screens the flow needs but the PDF did not include (marked NEW). Each screen lists its route, so it maps straight onto the Next.js app folder.

App flow

Two separate apps. Customer app tabs: Explore, Search, Saved, Profile (Buy/Rent toggle in the top bar). Owner app tabs: Dashboard, Listings, Post (centre button), Inquiries, Account.
